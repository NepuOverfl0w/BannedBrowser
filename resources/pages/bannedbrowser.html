BannedBrowser_

A web browser hacked together from scratch in Rust. No engines borrowed, no rules followed.

Version {{VERSION}} · github.com/imm0nv1nhtv/BannedBrowser · Releases

Most "new browsers" are a skin on Chromium, Gecko or WebKit. BannedBrowser writes the web platform itself: URL parsing, HTTP, HTML, DOM, CSS, style, layout, painting, the browser chrome and this very page are all its own code. Third-party crates are limited to platform glue such as windows, fonts, image codecs and TLS, and the build fails if a borrowed engine sneaks in.

What we wrote ourselves

WHATWG URL parser, IDNA, Punycode
HTTP/1.1 client: keep-alive, chunked, redirects, proxies, data:/file:/about:
HTML tokenizer and tree builder, DOM
CSS parser, selectors, media queries, cascade, calc(), var()
Block, inline, float, table, flex, positioned layout; frames
Anti-aliased rasterizer, gradients, shadows, clipping, glyph cache
The browser chrome toolkit and its icons

Why "BannedBrowser"?

The name is a nod to Silver Wolf (银狼, Yín Láng) from Honkai: Star Rail, whose handle is @Banned_Player. She is a member of the Stellaron Hunters and a genius hacker who has mastered "Aether Editing" to tamper with the data of reality, and who views the universe as a massive immersive simulation game.

It fits the project's spirit: a browser that bans borrowed engines and builds every piece of the web platform from scratch, just to see if it can.
