# Code of conduct

BannedBrowser wants to be a friendly place to build a browser from scratch, whoever you are
and whatever your experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Discuss code and ideas, not people. Critique constructively and accept critique gracefully.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first, and keep reports focused.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, in any form.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn, suspend or ban people who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
